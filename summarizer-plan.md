# Clinical Summary Improvement Plan

The preparation work is complete for review: the proposed summary workflow has central scoring rules for Safety, Severity, Urgency, Criticality, and risk bands; safer VALD handling using valid trial averages and bilateral asymmetry calculation; protection against treating missing or zero readings as patient deficits; clearer separation between patient concerns, missing information, and programme recommendations; and checks to detect if important concern scores or numbers change between the detailed report and dashboard summary. The `new-summary-dev` collection is available for safe development testing and comparison without changing production summaries in `new-summary`.

Before any production change, we need clinical approval of what should count as an Area of Concern, how scores should be assigned, how VALD asymmetry and single-session data should be interpreted, and what evidence and dates must appear in the summary. After this approval, we will implement the agreed rules, test representative patient cases only in `new-summary-dev`, compare the results with current summaries, and ask the doctors to approve the output before a controlled production rollout.
